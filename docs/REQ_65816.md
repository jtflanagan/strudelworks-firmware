# Requirements: the 65816 card (soft core, L1, L2)

Status: **EXTRACTED 2026-09-18, branch `core_refactor`, from fw 2a5cbe1 / host 8fa0b0b**
(the ddr3_write_level tip, merged to master the same day). No RTL changed. Last IIgs bitstream on
record: a draw (docs/DLINE.md "The fronts' timing is a draw": the same source built twice,
+0.001 and -0.131); the card holds the earlier working bit.

This document is the set of requirements a from-first-principles redesign of the soft core, L1
and the L2 page cache must meet **for the 65816 card**: the card for the Apple IIgs, and for
turn-a-IIe-into-a-GS. Its sibling is `docs/REQ_65C02.md` (the legacy Apple II card, which must
fit the Alchitry Au). The two are separate projects by the user's decision (2026-09-18). **Items
common to both cards are stated in full in REQ_65C02.md and referenced here by its section
number**, so they cannot drift; this document holds only what is different or additional for the
65816 card. Tags: **[C]** common (see the sibling), **[816]** this card only.

**Terminology.** The layer between the soft core and the L2 page cache is **L1**: L1-I (today
rtl/iline.sv + rtl/iscan.sv) and L1-D (today rtl/dline.sv). "Front" and its compounds are retired.

**The board.** This card is the Alchitry Pt V2 (xc7a100tfgg484-2: 63,400 LUTs, 135 RAMB36). The
GS build today is 37.6k LUTs routed (40.2k in the 2026-09-18 build) and 110-112 RAMB36
(build/results/utilization_hier.rpt): L1-D 5.4k, L1-I 4.1k, page_cache 3.2k + 64 RAMB36, gs_io
5.5k + 19 RAMB36 (the DOC 3.7k + 16), card_link 7.5k + 11, cpu65816 1.5k + 8. There is room, and
no fit constraint beyond timing; the constraint on this card is the route-bound closure of §3.6.
**The L1 and L2 decisions of this card are its own** (user, 2026-09-18): the 65C02 card is scaling
both back to fit the Au, and neither project takes the other's cache design as a given, though each
may learn from the other's measurements and share ideas.

## 1. System-level rules

All of REQ_65C02.md §1 applies, with these differences:

**1.1 The figure of merit [C]**: a 2.8 MHz IIgs does 2.8 M bus cycles/s; the target ~100 M/s is a
~36x multiplier (docs/UCODE816.md:19-30).

**1.2 There is no mode 0 [816].** On this card the accelerator is mode 2 with serving on, always
(rtl/wb_regfile.sv:1327; the user, 2026-09-15): the board sits in a //e whose bus cannot carry a
24-bit address, so reading or writing RAM on the bus is nonsense. The 65816 is held in reset out
of any card-level reset (power-on, bitstream load, link reset, watchdog) until the host has pushed
every ROM chunk and each has drained (rom_ok), then starts at its reset vector
(rtl/master_top.sv:695-710). An Apple RES' does not reset the FPGA and does not hold. **Leaving
acceleration (§1.6 of the sibling) has nothing to leave to here.**

**1.3 No stolen cycles [C]** applies unchanged: the //e backplane's bus cycles are real time.

**1.4 What is slow is a matter of class alone [C]**, plus on this card: the IIgs chipset's own
registers (rtl/gs_io.sv: ADB, the clock, the DOC, the IWM, the interrupt controller) are answered
on the card; their side-effect-free reads are served by L1-D in any bank the I/O page is in
(io_vec, rtl/dline.sv:850-855); their claims are decided on the live address for the dispatcher
and on the registered op for the byte mux (rtl/master_top.sv:1010-1016).

**1.5 The governor [C]**, plus: **$C036 bit 7 is the machine's own speed switch** and is wired
straight into the target: clear = the program believes it is on a 1 MHz machine, target exactly
1.0, EXCEPT when the host says unlimited (ProDOS 8 clears the bit and Nox sat at 1.1x)
(rtl/master_top.sv:1174-1188).

**1.10 A DDR3-free boot path [C]** applies unchanged, and matters more here: the GS card cannot
run at all until the host has pushed 256 KB of ROM into the DDR3, so a calibration failure must
be visible over USB3 before that push is attempted.

**1.8 Timing verdict [C]**: the GS build has NO depth baseline of its own (build/wns.sh prints the
census but names nothing new); the redesign should give it one.

## 2. The soft core (cpu65816, accel_seq, the bus driver)

### 2.1 The interface contract

REQ_65C02.md §2.1.1-2.1.5 apply (one uop = one cycle, cancel consumes nothing, two din ports,
register-direct exports, interrupts). Widened and added:

**2.1.1 The 24-bit interface [816]** (rtl/cpu65816.sv:93-170): addr[23:0] = {bank, high, low};
vda/vpa (both high on a fetch); vpb active high; emu; cyc_is_i = VPA with a sim assertion that a
VPA cycle's address is PC-sourced; pc_addr[23:0] = {pbr, pch, pcl}; d_pred[23:0] with
**d_pred_bcls in {ZERO, DBR, OTHER}**; dbr_o; **bank_wr** = one registered clock after an edge
that wrote DBR or PBR, covering the clock between the write and map_changed when the next fetch
is already at the new PBR while every L1 valid bit still describes the old one
(rtl/master_top.sv:845-852). L1-D keeps one valid bit per line per class and never compares bank
bytes in a serve cone (docs/LONG_DATA.md "The problem").

**2.1.2 A cycle with VDA and VPA both low is not an access and its address means nothing
[816]** (rtl/cpu65816.sv:81-90, rtl/master_top.sv:827-841, docs/GS.md rung 3e). The part drives
R/W low through the internal cycle of a read-modify-write, and $C0xx reads are strobes; taken
off the pins the bench asked for block $0203 instead of 3 (a write) and BRKed on $C083 (a read).
The board parks it at $000000 as a read. The 816 harness uses VDA as the write enable and fails
any write the reference does not make. Not implemented: MLB, ABORT.

**2.1.3 Interrupts [816 additions]**: vectors are mode-dependent; PBR is forced to zero on the
vector fetch (rtl/cpu65816.sv:1237-1241); **a vector pull comes from the ROM** when the language
card is in RAM-read (cpu_bank = $FF on vpb unless SHADOW inhibits; rtl/master_top.sv:978-1003),
done at the one place the bank is formed so the host's mirror sees it; without it the machine
dies on its first interrupt. In emulation mode bit 4 of the pushed P is set for BRK, COP and PHP
and clear only for a hardware interrupt.

**2.1.4 Reset [816]**: emulation mode, 8-bit registers, S = $01FF, I set, entering the interrupt
sequence at slot 2 with uaddr forced to {$00, 2} while rst holds so the pipeline is primed
(rtl/cpu65816.sv:1012-1035). **No reset sequence rides the bus** (it would fetch the IIe's
vector; rtl/accel_seq.sv:568-577).

### 2.2 The sequencer's contract

REQ_65C02.md §2.2.1-2.2.8 and §2.2.10 apply. Differences:

**2.2.1 The dummies are this card's only path to the IIe keyboard [816]**
(rtl/master_top.sv:945-956): a physical GS keyboard is ADB (rtl/gs_adb.sv, fed by the host).

**2.2.2 The internal cycle rides S_NULL [816]** (go_null = GS && cyc_int; rtl/accel_seq.sv:490),
three clocks on the dispatcher (settle, decide, null), since fixed to one clock on L1-D (§3.1.6).

**2.2.3 Map-moving writes on this card [816]**: $C02D SLOTROMSEL, $C035 SHADOW, $C068 STATEREG
hold the settle (gs_map_wr, rtl/accel_seq.sv:472-474); $C036 CYAREG is explicitly NOT (the map
does not read it).

**2.2.4 Overrides [816]**: the driver's ovr_en is gated by sl_win so a DMA is never presented
inside an on-card slot op's window (rtl/master_top.sv:903-910).

**2.2.5 Virtual time is WALL time [816]**: vbeam runs every bus cycle, never a fast tick
(docs/VIRTUAL_TIME.md:101-114, docs/GS.md:3700-3742), because a real IIgs accelerator cannot
speed up the 1 MHz bus; double-click stopped working when it did.

**2.2.6 The bank on the unified stream [816]**: cpu_bank rides the stream and is latched with the
op so the classifier and the event record see the same 24 bits; an override's last cycle retires
after ovr_en has dropped and must still be stamped with the bank it had (dq_bank held, not
sampled); u_bank = op_ovr ? dq_bank : bus_bank, because stamping zero wrote bank $7E's DMA into
bank zero over zero page and the stack (rtl/accel_seq.sv:607).

**2.2.7 The dispatcher's price is the same 7.7 clocks per op [C]**, and on this card the
histogram says where it goes: on the Nox map at 33.9x PLAIN, RETIRE 47.6%, **DEC_D 23.6%**,
SEQ_FAST 11.3%, DEC_I 7.4%, IFILL 6.7%, DFILL 2.7% (docs/GS.md:3760-3772); 45 M dispatcher ops
cost ~350 M clocks. "The IIe does 60x on this map because it sends FEWER ops there."

### 2.3 Functional invariants: what the oracles pin

**2.3.1 SingleStepTests/65816, 981,610 cases [816]** (rtl/cpu65816.sv:7-13): every cycle's
address, direction, data, VDA/VPA/VPB, the count, and the state after, native and emulated, with
25% random cancels and garbage on both din ports; cycle structure separately held by
sim/gen_cycle_table816.py (1803 (opcode, mode) buckets; MVP/MVN/WAI/STP excluded). Not covered:
reset, IRQ, NMI, ABORT, MLB, a (dp) pointer at $FF with DL zero in emulation. The FULL run has
never been done. The suite carries no statement of how it was produced: a very good second
opinion, not silicon. **There is no functional oracle for the 816** beyond this and boot_gs's
KEGS floor.

**2.3.2 Interrupt entry holds PC across the two incrementing cycles [C]**, learned here from
GS/OS: every interrupt returned two bytes late, into a JSL's operand (a $00, so a BRK)
(rtl/cpu65816.sv:800-805, docs/GS.md rung 4c). cpu816_irq_tb carries it, both modes.

**2.3.3 Decimal [816]**: no extra cycle exists; the adjust runs in the pass; decimal ADC takes V
from the sum after the LOW nibble's adjust and before the high one, which is what the suite
shows (rtl/cpu65816.sv:552-578); decimal SBC keeps binary V.

**2.3.4 The KEGS floor [816]**: boot_gs must not diverge from the KEGS-derived trace sooner than
event 67,398 (sim/suite.sh gs). The map, the vector-pull redirect, SHADOW and STATEREG are
written from KEGS moremem.c, not from the RTL (that independence found three real bugs).

**2.3.5 Carry gift and no global CE [C]** apply; the second was learned on this core (+6.5 MHz).

### 2.5 Accumulated complexity not to carry [816]

- Five copies of the address-source mux (addr_mux, d_pred_mux, pred_hi/pred_bk, adh_nx, s_nx)
  held consistent by six (* keep *) nets and a runtime $error (rtl/cpu65816.sv:425-464, 830-983).
- The early-carry adder: a second adder reading the prefetched word's lo_b field for a purpose it
  was not defined for, plus seq_ir = f_sync ? fetch_op : ir (rtl/cpu65816.sv:706, 744-765):
  "exactly the kind of special-case hardware the original part's random control logic is made of."
- Three ROMs (ucode, ucode0 for slot 0, useq0 for slot 0's cond/alt) and two assemblers that
  share nothing with the 65C02's; roughly a third of the file's comment volume is dated timing

## 3. L1

REQ_65C02.md §3.1.1, 3.1.3, 3.1.6, 3.1.7, all of §3.2, §3.3.1, 3.3.2, 3.3.4, 3.3.5, §3.4 and the
house laws of §3.6 apply. Differences and additions:

**3.1.2 Zero-stall branch serve is NOT built on this card [816, a target]**: its select would be
a live 16-bit compare on the PC feeding the 816's decode-ROM / block-RAM address loop, so i_din
must be register-direct here (rtl/iline.sv:458-466). A taken 65816 branch spends its add cycle as
an internal cycle anyway (docs/GS.md:3513-3518).

**3.1.4 A data read into a held line retires in TWO clocks [816]** (rtl/dline.sv:942, 866-885);
the hit and byte are registered at the end of the first clock and retire on the second. Writes
keep one clock. **This second clock is the thing to fix.** Six servers as the sibling, plus: the
last row serves the OTHER bank class the ways refuse; the keyboard serve is $C000 in the pair
banks from the ADB micro's byte; the chipset fast reads (io_hit) in any bank the I/O page is in.

**3.1.5 Writes [816]**: below $C000 in bank zero, OR anywhere in banks $02-$7F (w_hi,
rtl/dline.sv:921); pair-bank long (OTHER-class) writes and LC writes ride the dispatcher because
they name the map and SHADOW. **The shadow duplicate is made by L1-D itself**: a write into a
shadowed region reloads the splice register the next clock with the same byte at
{$E0 | side, page}, so every coherence rule applies to the duplicate unchanged; a retire on that
clock is held one clock; the FIFO is 8 deep with the room sample three entries short
(rtl/dline.sv:1088-1093, docs/GS.md "gs_post").

**3.1.6 A 65816 internal cycle retires on L1-D in ONE clock [816]** with no address and no stream
event (n_cycle, rtl/dline.sv:901, 945): one core cycle in five on Nox's loop.

**3.1.8 The bank class serve [816]** (docs/LONG_DATA.md): each line keeps two precomputed valid
bits, cvz (its virtual bank is zero, set at install) and cvd (it is the DBR as it stands,
recomputed on every map change); a ZERO or DBR candidate serves by dq_b0 ? cvz : (dq_d && cvd)
beside the valid bit, no bank byte in the cone. OTHER-class candidates (absolute long, [dp],
[dp],Y, MVN/MVP, an index across a bank edge, PBR data with PBR non-zero) are served by a
**two-entry bank table** with LRU and a per-line-per-entry bit cvo (rung 1), posted in banks
$02-$7F (rung 2), and the chipset's fast reads in any pair bank (rung 3); the census that sized
it: GS/OS 19% of data cycles are OTHER memory, 94.9% of them in the last two distinct banks; XMAS
7.2% / 99.8%. On the card: 81% of OTHER reads served, Arkanoid 77x against 52-56x.

**3.3.3 What map_changed covers on this card [816]**: PBR, DBR, SHADOW ($C035), SLOTROMSEL
($C02D), STATEREG ($C068), CYAREG bit 4 (all-banks shadow), beside the IIe set; map_now is a
53-bit word (rtl/apple_mmu.sv:1285). front_settling (to be l1_settling) = map_settling ||
cpu_bank_wr. The pair banks ($00/$01/$E0/$E1) are where the map applies; the $Cxxx exclusions of
§3.1.7 apply in those four banks alone, and in bank $FF $C3xx is ROM code and $CFFF an ordinary
byte (rtl/iline.sv:484-490).

| the Nox histogram | above, §2.2.7 | |

**CAPACITY is the named unresolved item on this card**: "a toolbox working set that does not fit
a ten-line L1"; capacity is its likely answer and was never built (the 16x4 attempt above).

**3.6 Timing [816]**: the draw of 2026-09-18 (docs/DLINE.md:68-105): the fanout limits on L1-I's
CAM registers (want_tag, f_vtag, f_ppn at max_fanout 24: the worst path was 3.3 ns logic + 7.0
ns route, fanouts 157 and 104) and on L1-D's sh_we/sh_waddr (348 loads) took -0.359 to about
-0.1; removing the DDR3 island helped; everything after sits in a 0.1 ns band narrower than
build-to-build spread. Both worst paths at every stage were three quarters ROUTE, in different
modules: congestion, not depth. The recommendation on record: a region constraint for L1, or a
structural cut worth more than a tenth of a nanosecond.

**3.7 Accumulated complexity [816]**: GS threaded through both L1 sides as ~30 separate
conditionals rather than a parameterisation (t_vbank, pair_q, tgt_serve, f_vbank, rv_ok
branches; dq_b0/dq_d/dq_wsh/dq_kbd/dq_o, w_hi, n_cycle, sv_hit_q, a different d_din expression,
PW_DEPTH, pf_near_full, dup_go, spl_sh/spl_dup, cvz/cvd/cvo, rv_pair/rv_bank, the ob_* table,
io_bank/io_hit/kb_hit); the bank table as a cache-within-a-cache (a 64-flop per-line bit
rewritten by a triple nested loop the clock after a replacement, eligibility exclusions for I/O
and ROM banks); the pair-bank test written five times; front_bypass (a chicken bit ISTREAM.md
said there would never be) for this card only. On a card with one machine, all of this is either
the design or gone.

**3.8 Test obligations [816]**: boot_gs at the floor with the $E0 duplicate checks (9) and the
bank-table books; gs_io_claim (18,439 checks from the KEGS rule); mmu_xlate_gs (3,584 maps,
67.1 M checks, 0); GS/OS 12/0 and XMAS 11/0 at 20 M instructions; the IIe battery is no longer a
parity check for this card once the trees split.

## 4. The L2 page cache and the MMU

REQ_65C02.md §4.2.1-4.2.3, 4.2.5-4.2.8, all of §4.3, §4.4.1, 4.4.3, §4.5, §4.6, §4.8 and
§4.10's common benches apply. Differences and additions:

**4.1 The physical space on this card [816]** (rtl/apple_mmu.sv:213-236, 926-1066): $00-$7F RAM
(what the ROM sizes), $E0/$E1 the Mega II banks, $FC-$FF ROM 3. ABOVE $7F THE MAP IS OURS
(docs/ENHANCED_VIDEO.md R0, 2026-09-26; the "void bank" was KEGS's model of a machine without
the memory, and this card has it): RAM $80-$DF (the enhanced video banks) and $E2-$EF ($E2 the
video control bank, $EF the panel's RAM), ROM space $F0-$FF (GS_ROM_LO; the panel at $F0,
docs/GS_CONTROL_PANEL.md). Every bank is memory; the host mirror carries the same (host
src/apple_memory.cpp classify_gs).

**4.2.4 Geometry [816]**: 128x8 = 256 KB or larger; the Pt has the block RAM. **ROM 3 is 256 KB
= the cache, hashes to exactly 8 pages in every one of the 128 sets, and because the loader
writes it through the posted seam all 1024 pages are DIRTY FOREVER**: every RAM page the machine
touches evicts a dirty ROM page and pays a 16-beat write-back of bytes that cannot have changed
(docs/GS.md:1029-1045; a GS/OS boot: 1,690 fills, 665 whole-page flushes). Identified, costed,
not fixed: **the single largest known defect on this card.**

**4.3 A fourth entrant kind, the shadow duplicate [816]** (shd_take, rtl/page_cache.sv:106-117):
the duplicate needs a P0 of its own; without it every duplicate was a ppn_clash (56 of 56); it
must also wait for pw_ready, or a duplicate offered one clock behind an op that just missed is
silently gone (caught by boot_gs once in a million instructions). shd_drop counts the case and a
bench asserts zero. mwx_stb/mw_bank is the any-bank write tap gs_io uses to watch the SCBs land
at $E1/9D00.

**4.4.2 The GS map [816]** (rtl/apple_mmu.sv:473-511, 732-777, 926-1066; from KEGS moremem.c):
$C035 SHADOW's veto follows where the write LANDS, not which bank the CPU named; each region has a
main and an aux answer picked by bsel or by the odd bank named directly; $C036 bit 4 shadows odd
banks $03+'s $2000-$9FFF into $E1 (Arkanoid relocated into bank $07 drew through it; bit 23 keeps
our banks from $80 out); $C035 bit 6 SWAPS the I/O and LC windows (CPU $C000-$CFFF reads the image's
$D000, CPU $D000-$DFFF the image's $C000, fold off); $C068 STATEREG moves seven switches at once;
$C02D SLOTROMSEL per slot applied after the table (bit 3 IS SLOTC3ROM); $C071-$C07F reads are the
ROM's, a byte-level override whose ordering is load-bearing; CYAREG is reset by the card's rst
alone (bit 6 is the power-on status ROM 3 reads). 3,584 maps, 67.1 M checks, 0.

**4.5 The ROM loader on this card [816]**: 512 chunks, ROM_PPN0 = $FC00; absolute chunks land in
$F0-$FB for the panel and never satisfy rom_ok; the load runs only while the CPU is held
(REQUIRE_HOLD), acked per chunk, and the host skips the push when MEM_STAT says the ROM is there.

**4.6 Reset [816]**: the 65816 is held out of any card reset until rom_ok (§1.2); RES' does not
touch the cache, the ROM, CYAREG, or the DDR3.

**4.9 Complexity [816]**: the GS_* parameter family (GS_ROM_LO, GS_CYAREG_POR) and the "IIe netlist bit-identical at GS=0" parity
gate on every one of them; once the trees split the gate is the IIe card's own battery on its
own tree.

**4.10 Test obligations [816]**: mmu_xlate_gs; boot_gs's rung-4c stale-line audit (every
never-written byte in banks 0/1 equals its power-up value in BOTH the cache and the DDR3, on a
ROM boot and an 8 M-instruction GS/OS boot: 0 wrong), ppn/land clash 0, shd_drops 0, the
shadow-duplicate byte watch on $07F7 / $E0/07F7, SmartPort blocks through posted writes; the mirror
replay, fw tools/gs_mirror_test.sh driving the host's gs_memory_test (1,817,727 ops, 481 pages,
zero bytes different).

## 5. What the list says (observations, not design)

REQ_65C02.md §5 applies. For this card in particular:

1. **The two-clock data read and the 3-deep microcode pipe are this card's problem alone** now:
   the 816's early-carry adder, its five mux copies and its BRAM-fed slot 0 exist to reach one
   clock per microinstruction at 100 MHz with a byte that arrives 5.6 ns into the clock. Whether
   the serve latency should be one clock at the core's din or one clock at a register the core
   reads next cycle is the central question, and on this card it is asked of a 24-bit AGU.
2. **Capacity for GS/OS's working set is the named target** (§3.5), and the 16x4 attempt is the
   warning: it met timing and would not calibrate, for a reason nobody found.
3. **The ROM's permanently dirty pages** (§4.2.4) are a defect with a known fix (mark the
   loader's pages clean) waiting on the redesign.
4. **Congestion, not depth, is what closes or fails this card** (§3.6): a region constraint for
   L1 is the tool the numbers point at; a structural cut has to be worth more than a tenth of a
   nanosecond.
5. **Carried forward as targets, not requirements**: the flat 24-bit map input (the map is the
